# Gooey.AI

> Gooey.AI is a low-code, open-source AI orchestration platform for building, evaluating and deploying multilingual AI agents, including in low-resource languages. Impact organizations and governments use it to deliver AI services in agriculture, health, education and culture, on infrastructure they can control.

**Platform.** Gooey.AI is model-agnostic: teams use private and open-source models from many providers through one account and one bill, and can chain speech recognition, language models and text-to-speech into a single workflow. Agents can be deployed to WhatsApp, IVR, Android, Slack, Facebook and the web, or embedded in any site or app, and every workflow is available through the API. Gooey.AI is SOC2 Type II certified (audited by Scrut Automation) and GDPR and HIPAA compliant. Neither Gooey.AI nor its AI providers train on your data.

**Sovereignty.** Gooey Server, the core of the platform, is open source under the Apache 2.0 license and can be self-hosted on-premises, in a private cloud or air-gapped, so organizations can keep data and AI workloads within their own country or infrastructure. It runs local and open models through any OpenAI-compatible server such as Ollama or vLLM, and every proprietary dependency has a documented open alternative, so there is no lock-in to a single cloud or AI provider. In a position paper, Gooey.AI's founders argue that middle powers should cooperate on AI sovereignty by shaping AI benchmarks, publishing representative datasets and keeping models swappable through open orchestration layers.

**Impact.** Gooey.AI is the platform and partner for organizations building multilingual, voice-first AI agents that work in low-connectivity settings and reach people with or without smartphones. Agents are grounded in vetted guidelines, documents and videos, and include analytics to track outcomes. Work includes agricultural advisory agents with the Gates Foundation and Digital Green (Farmer.CHAT), health assistants with UN IOM and the MEXA Generative AI for Mental Health Accelerator, a voice agent with the City of Seattle, an AI teaching assistant with Teach For India, and inclusive generative AI with the Goethe-Institut (Beyond Bias).

**Low-resource languages.** With the support of the Gates Foundation and CLEAR Global, Gooey.AI publishes evaluations of how well private and open-source speech and language models answer real health, agriculture and education questions spoken in underrepresented languages, including Swahili, Kikuyu, Kinyarwanda, Urdu, Pashto, Sindhi, Hausa, Nigerian Fulfulde, Igbo, Yoruba and Nigerian Pidgin. Native speakers record the questions, experts write reference answers, and a published LLM judge scores each answer's accuracy alongside end-to-end latency. Every evaluation, prompt, model setting and workflow is public and forkable, so organizations can rerun them on their own data.
